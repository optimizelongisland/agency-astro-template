Most sprinkler systems are set to run more often than the lawn actually needs. Overwatering doesn't just waste money — it encourages shallow roots, fungus, and weaker grass overall.

## The short answer

Most lawns need about 1 to 1.5 inches of water per week, including rainfall, split across 2 to 3 sessions rather than a daily short cycle. Deep, infrequent watering trains roots to grow down instead of staying near the surface.

## Adjust by season

- **Spring and fall**: 2 sessions per week is usually enough
- **Peak summer**: 3 sessions per week, early morning to reduce evaporation
- **After heavy rain**: skip the next scheduled run entirely

## Signs you're overwatering

If you're seeing mushy spots, mushroom growth, or a spongy feel underfoot, cut back before adjusting anything else. A smart controller that factors in local rainfall automatically removes most of the guesswork here.
