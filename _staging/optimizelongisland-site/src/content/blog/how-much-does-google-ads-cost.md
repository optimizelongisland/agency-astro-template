Google Ads has two costs. The first is ad spend, which you pay directly to Google. Most local businesses start between $1,000 and $3,000 a month. The second is the management fee. At Optimize Long Island that is $500 to set up ($515 by credit card) and $500 a month ($515) for ad budgets up to $3,000 a month. For larger budgets the fee is 15% of ad spend, and never less than $500.

So a Long Island business spending $2,000 a month on ads would pay $2,000 to Google and $500 to us, plus the one-time $500 setup. What decides the right budget for you is what a new customer is worth, and we talk that through in the free consultation. The listed prices are for check, cash or bank transfer, and credit cards add a 3% fee, shown in dollars beside each price.

## How much does Google Ads cost a local business?

There are two separate bills, and it helps to keep them apart.

| Cost | Who you pay | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Ad spend | Google | Your choice. Most local businesses start between $1,000 and $3,000 a month. |
| Setup fee | Optimize Long Island | $500 one time ($515 by credit card) |
| Management fee | Optimize Long Island | $500 a month ($515 by credit card) for ad budgets up to $3,000 a month. For larger budgets, 15% of ad spend, and never less than $500. |

Google bills your ad spend to you directly. You can see the setup and management fees with the rest of our services in the [Google Ads prices](/pricing/#ads), or read about our [Google Ads management](/services/ppc/).

For a worked example, a $2,000 monthly budget means $2,000 in ad spend and a $500 management fee, for a total of $2,500 a month: $2,000 to Google and $500 to us ($515 by credit card). Ad spend is paid to Google, never to us, and monthly plans are paid automatically by bank transfer or card.

## How do you decide on an ad budget?

There is no fixed answer. Most local businesses start between $1,000 and $3,000 a month in ad spend, and where you land inside or outside that range depends on a few things you already know about your business.

Start with the value of a lead. If a typical new customer is worth a few hundred dollars to you, you can afford to pay more for each enquiry than a business whose jobs are worth much less. Then think about the season. A pool company on the South Shore and a snow removal contractor want their ads running at different times of year, and the budget should follow the demand.

We do not quote cost-per-click figures here because they vary by service, by town and by month, and a made-up number would only mislead you. We go over it with you in the free consultation.

## What does the setup fee pay for?

The $500 setup fee ($515 by credit card) pays for the groundwork that makes the ads measurable.

- **Account structure.** How your campaigns and ad groups are organized, so each service has ads that match what people search for.
- **Conversion and call tracking.** Set up so that a form submission or a phone call counts as a lead and can be traced back to the ad that produced it.
- **A landing page.** A page built for the ad, so someone who clicks arrives where you want them to act.

Tracking matters most. Without it you are paying for clicks and guessing at the result.

## What does the monthly fee pay for?

The monthly fee covers the ongoing work of running the account.

Every week we check spend, cost per lead and wasted search terms. A wasted search term is a search that triggered your ad but was never going to become a customer, such as someone looking for a job in your trade. Finding them means you can stop paying for them.

We also check Google's own conversion count against the leads you really received. Ad platforms and real life do not always agree, and the difference tells you whether the tracking is right.

AI does the analysis and drafting. A person sets the direction and approves every change to your ad account. You get a monthly report in plain English with the numbers and what we did. See [what we report](/reporting/).

## Is Google Ads worth it?

It depends on what a lead is worth to you and on whether the tracking is set up properly. Google Ads can put your business in front of people at the moment they search for what you sell. But you pay for clicks whether or not the visitor becomes a customer.

That is why we set up conversion and call tracking first. With it, you can compare what you spend with the leads you receive and decide for yourself. Without it, any answer is a guess. We do not promise results. We show you the numbers each month so you can see whether it is paying for itself.
