## What makes one website cost more than another?

Four things move the price, and you can see all of them in the table above.

**The number of pages.** A plumber with five pages (home, services, about, contact and one more) needs less than a contractor with a page for every service and every town.

**Who writes the words.** On a Launch Website we use your own answers and materials. The Growth Website includes copywriting from a short interview with you.

**Whether there is an old site to move.** Moving an existing site with redirects, so each old address points to its new one and your Google rankings carry over, is part of the Growth Website.

**What the site has to do.** A site that explains what you do and collects enquiries is the simple case. Online booking, customer logins, databases and e-commerce are a different kind of build.

## What is not included in the price?

A Launch Website does not include copywriting from scratch, logo design, photography or e-commerce. If you need a logo or photos, you supply them. For a Launch Website we ask for your answers to a short questionnaire, your logo and your photos, or a shared folder of them.

Ad spend and SEO are separate services. A website brings no visitors by itself. If you want help getting found, we offer [SEO](/services/seo/), Google Ads management and social media ads management, each with its own published price.
