Listed prices are for check, cash or bank transfer, and credit cards add a 3% fee.

For $750 we run your Google Business Profile and weekly posts, build and clean up your business listings, optimize your pages and write two pieces of content a month. For $1,500 we add more content, new service and location pages, link building, review generation, and tracking of how your business shows up in AI answers. Nobody can honestly promise a ranking, and we do not. What we do is report your results every month so you can see what is happening.

## What do you get for $750 a month?

The Local SEO plan covers the basics a local business needs to be found on Google and on Google Maps.

- **Google Business Profile optimization and weekly posts.** We fill in and maintain your free Google listing and post to it every week. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization/) shows what that involves.
- **Business listings built, cleaned up and kept consistent.** Your name, address and phone number should match everywhere they appear online.
- **On-page optimization.** We tune the pages of your site so Google can tell what you do and where.
- **Two new pieces of content a month.** Pages or posts that answer the questions your customers search for.
- **A press release up to once a quarter,** when you have real news to share.
- **A monthly report** in plain English.

## What does $1,500 a month add?

Growth SEO includes everything in Local SEO and adds more of the work that builds visibility over time.

- Four pieces of content a month instead of two.
- New service and location pages, so each thing you do and each town you serve has its own page.
- Link building, which means getting other sites to link to yours.
- Review generation, so more of your customers leave a review.
- Tracking of how your business shows up in AI answers on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity. You can read more on our [AI search visibility page](/services/ai-search-visibility/).
- Competitor tracking.
- A quarterly strategy call.

If your business sells a few services in a handful of Long Island towns, Local SEO is the plan to look at first. If you want to cover many services and towns, or compete harder, Growth SEO is built for that.

## Why do some businesses pay more?

Two situations push the price up: more than one location, or a highly competitive market. Both mean more pages to build, more listings to manage and more work to be seen against other businesses. We start those engagements at $2,500 a month ($2,575 by credit card) and quote them after a free audit, so the price reflects your actual situation and not a guess.

The audit looks at where you stand now and what it would take.

## How do you know the money is working?

You should be able to see it. Our monthly report uses your own Google data: clicks, impressions and average position for the searches that find you, compared with the same month a year earlier. A year-over-year comparison matters because most local businesses are seasonal, and a month-to-month comparison can mislead you.

We also report visits to your site and where they came from, leads from your form and tracked calls where they are set up, and Google Business Profile results: calls, direction requests, website clicks and views. See [what the monthly report shows](/reporting/) for the full list.

Results take time and depend on your market. A report that shows the real numbers is how you decide whether to keep going.

## Can I do local SEO myself instead?

Partly, yes. You can claim and complete your Google Business Profile, ask every customer for a review and reply to the ones you get. Those cost nothing but time, and our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization/) walks through them.

What takes sustained effort is the rest: keeping listings consistent, writing new pages, building links and checking your numbers every month. That is where owners usually run out of hours. If you are not sure, start with the checklist and call us when you want help.
